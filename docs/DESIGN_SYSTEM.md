# Design System

The revised direction is Silicon Valley spatial-tech: deep OLED surfaces, precise luminous accents, 3D spatial systems, product-platform language and immersive motion. JEF should read as a technology company specializing in AI and Extended Reality, with education, training and skills development at the centre.

Tokens are centralized in `src/styles/globals.css` and mapped into Tailwind in `tailwind.config.ts`.

Provisional palette:

- Ink: OLED off-black
- Surface: dark product-panel navy
- Paper: luminous near-white
- Chalk: muted technical grey
- Cyan: primary spatial intelligence accent
- Lime: secondary learning-signal accent
- Canopy: restrained compute-blue
- Graphite: readable neutral text

Typography:

- Display: Space Grotesk
- Interface/body: Geist
- Technical/data: Geist Mono

Component guidance:

- Avoid unchanged shadcn visuals.
- Avoid generic purple AI gradients, stock SaaS dashboards, unchanged shadcn components and repetitive card sections.
- Use cards only for meaningful repeated objects.
- Prefer immersive 3D, product modules, spatial systems, technical evidence displays and motion that explains how capability is captured, modeled and deployed.
